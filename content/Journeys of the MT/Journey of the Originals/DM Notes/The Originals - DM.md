# The Preparation

Make character cheats for every possible player, a marvel multiverse RPG (Rank 1) one and a D&D 5e one (Level 1) are needed.

# The Mysterious Person

## The Setup

When the players are ready to start playing, the game starts, but try to make it smooth, so they don't notice that the game actually started. If they would ask whether you actually started let them roll a d20, they might know the game started but that doesn't mean their character knows.

> **Examples:**
> Are you all ready for the game?
> Are there any expectations you have, any things I should not joke about or something?
> Which food will we order?

When you are ready to start for real you can talk about character sheets.

> As I told beforehand I prepared character sheets for all of you so here they...
> And just as I am about to hand out the character sheets, a purplish portal opens right at the door and a man looking exactly like me is coming out of it.

Here the players may decide what they will do, will they talk to the person? Fight him? If they decide to fight him they all roll initiative (D&D one) and fight against the person, the person is a human fighter on fifth level. If they manage to defeat him, he appears behind them.

> [[The Creator]]: Guys, before you try to attack, can I first explain myself.

They can fight him again, but this time he will be a level 6 sorcerer. After he is defeated this time, when he comes back there is nothing they can do. 

> Soon after his body disappears another version appears, this time he seems to emanate a godly glow.
> [[The Creator]]: Enough!
> The room fills itself with a green aura after which you feel yourself unable to move, you can talk but moving is impossible.

## Talking to the Creator

When they decide to talk to [[The Creator]] (or are forced to) they can ask him whatever they want. Some of the most obvious question are answered here.

> **Q**: Who are you?
> **A**: I am known under many names: Ingo, Ignatius Marenka, Sir Maréchal, Infinity. But I think it is best to announce myself as The Creator I guess.
> 
> **Q**: Where did you come from?
> **A**: I originate from Universe RSS-0001, a universe far from here, I mean relatively, for me everything is just forming a fist away.
> 
> **Q**: Why did you come here?
> **A**: Very good question, I am here because we need you. I have been looking at you through the eyes of this Ingo variant here. He is one of our agents in this universe.

When they are ready asking questions to [[The Creator]] he explains the mission to them more. 

> So, let me explain the situation somewhat more detailed. We at universe RSS-0001 have been working closely with your universe's SAC. Together they launched a project known as project S.I.M.P.L.E. a project that connects universes together and makes sure the multiverse stays as stable as possible. We tried finding some elite agents who could handle this Multiversal situation. Together with your Ignatius we discovered that TTRPG players would be perfect for this as you guys are kind off used to getting in a role. This Ingo here looked at you to see whether you are good people to do this and the results came out splendid. So what do you all say to some adventure?

The party will probably accept now and [[The Creator]] takes everyone except [[The Leader|their Ingo]] with him to universe [[RSS-0001]].
