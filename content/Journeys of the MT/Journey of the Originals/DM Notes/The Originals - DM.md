# The Preparation

Make character cheats for every possible player, a marvel multiverse RPG (Rank 1) one and a D&D 5e one (Level 1) are needed.

# The Mysterious Person

## The Setup

When the players are ready to start playing, the game starts, but try to make it smooth, so they don't notice that the game actually started. If they would ask whether you actually started let them roll a d20, they might know the game started but that doesn't mean their character knows.

> **Examples:**
> Are you all ready for the game?
> Are there any expectations you have, any things I should not joke about or something?
> Which food will we order?

When you are ready to start for real you can talk about character sheets.

> As I told beforehand I prepared character sheets for all of you so here they...
> And just as I am about to hand out the character sheets, a purplish portal opens right at the door and a man looking exactly like me is coming out of it.

Here the players may decide what they will do, will they talk to the person? Fight him? If they decide to fight him they all roll initiative (D&D one), receive their Dungeons and Dragons sheets and fight against the person, the person is a human fighter on fifth level. If they manage to defeat him, he appears behind them.

> [[The Creator]]: Guys, before you try to attack, can I first explain myself.

They can fight him again, but this time he will be a level 6 sorcerer. After he is defeated this time, when he comes back there is nothing they can do. 

> Soon after his body disappears another version appears, this time he seems to emanate a godly glow.
> [[The Creator]]: Enough!
> The room fills itself with a green aura after which you feel yourself unable to move, you can talk but moving is impossible.

## Talking to the Creator

When they decide to talk to [[The Creator]] (or are forced to) they can ask him whatever they want. Some of the most obvious question are answered here.

> **Q**: Who are you?
> **A**: I am known under many names: Ingo, Ignatius Marenka, Sir Maréchal, Infinity. But I think it is best to announce myself as The Creator I guess.
> 
> **Q**: Where did you come from?
> **A**: I originate from Universe RSS-0001, a universe far from here, I mean relatively, for me everything is just forming a fist away.
> 
> **Q**: Why did you come here?
> **A**: Very good question, I am here because we need you. I have been looking at you through the eyes of this Ingo variant here. He is one of our agents in this universe.

When they are ready asking questions to [[The Creator]] he explains the mission to them more. 

> So, let me explain the situation somewhat more detailed. I am Ignatius Marenka Maréchal and we at universe RSS-0001 have been working closely with your universe's SAC. Together they launched a project known as project S.I.M.P.L.E. a project that connects universes together and makes sure the multiverse stays as stable as possible. We tried finding some elite agents who could handle this Multiversal situation. Together with your Ignatius we discovered that TTRPG players would be perfect for this as you guys are kind off used to getting in a role. This Ingo here looked at you to see whether you are good people to do this and the results came out splendid. So what do you all say to some adventure?

The party will probably accept now and [[The Creator]] takes everyone except [[The Leader|their Ingo]] with him to universe [[RSS-0001]].

# The S.I.M.P.L.E.

It is now that the Marvel sheets will be distributed and used.

> You are all standing in a great white hall now. It has a walking platform around the room with occasional platforms connecting the left and the right side of the building. The space in the middle of the room is filled with white round shaped pods, all empty with nothing in it. In the death center there is a see-through sphere that is pumping with energy. It is connected by tubes to the little spheres. The middle one is way bigger, and glows a pulsing blue light.
> Ignatius is leaning against the railing that keeps travelers from falling down. He seems very comfortable leaning against it. 
> [[The Creator]]: Welcome to S.I.M.P.L.E., the Multiversal component of the Secret Agent Company. Here we try to make bonds with other universes in an attempt to keep everything balanced and safe.

When the players are ready to move on Ignatius takes them with him to some sort of Virtual training room. For the players it will look like an empty padded room with two doors, one through which they enter another one to somewhere unknown. He lines them up. Let them roll a d20. The number decides where their implanted S-gland is. 

> 1-2: Straight in the nuts
> 3-4: Stomach
> 5-6: Behind their ear
> 7-8: Legs
> 9-10: Feet
> 11-12:In their forehead
> 13-14: Arms
> 15-16: Hands
> 17-18: Chest
> 19-20: Already active

Their powers are now activated for good and can not be turned off.
It is now time for training. And Ignatius learns them a bit about their powers. After that he says he will check how they would do in the field and he starts a simulation.

> [[The Creator]]: Time for some real practice I guess. Hi-Tech, start test-protocol Originals.
> [[Hi-Tech]]: Yes sir, I am creating the effects now.
> All of a sudden the room changes and you now find yourself in a slightly lit alley. A lamp shimmering some light over the situation, Ignatius has disappeared and you find yourself against copies of yourself, except that those variants' eyes are blackened.

They then roll initiative and fight against their dark variants. Ignatius can occasionally throw in tips and tricks. After a successful battle they get back in the room and Ignatius appears again.

> Your dark variants are before you, defeated. All of a sudden the scene changes, you are back in the white room and [[The Creator]] is standing in front of you.
> [[The Creator]]: Great job everyone, I guess now it is time for the real work, don't you think. There should be a mission board in the main hall of the SAC building. Carl should be waiting for you guys there, he will guide you through the mission board. Then I am of now, I still have a lot of things to do, so I am off, good luck agents.
> And before you can ask any questions, the Creator makes a portal through which he vanishes.

# The way to the main hall

As the players exit the room they can come across many people who can help them, give them extra missions and so on. 

> You can draw a simple floorplan to know where all your characters are.

They easily can find their way back to the main place so no dice rolls needed there.
From the main S.I.M.P.L.E. hall will be five branches all situated on the lower floor. If they want to look for a floorplan (search), let them roll a vigilance check. On a successful (Difficulty to decide, but more difficult then average) roll they discover that on the walls are neon arrows with drawings. 

> One is some sort of tree, another is a flame, yet another is a spiral. The one you came from has three waves and the last one which looks slightly broader then the rest and is indicated with an infinity sign.

> [!INFO]
> Tree, Farran: Research department
> Flame, Inferno: Generator room
> Spiral, Tornado: Meeting rooms
> Waves, Waterno: Training rooms.
> Infinity sign, Infinity: Main hall

Now they can choose which way they go.
