# The beginning

## The stage

This one shot is meant for new members and fighters of the S.A.C. it will be played by every member of the Secret Agent Company and will be sort of a training for new Agents.

> You all are standing in a big room, you just got pulled through a portal, a thing you thought didn't exist.
> So you are standing in a big hall with the sunlight shining through the tall glass windows and can clearly see each other. The hall is easily 10 meter in height and 10 meter wide. Before you, on a small stage is a good looking guy. He is probably around 1m75 and is in his thirties.
> [[Karel|The Person]]: "Dear people, you probably are all wondering how you got up here? But answers will be here soon."
> [[Karel|The Person]] leaves and you are all left behind with only your thoughts.

This is when you teach your players a bit of roleplaying, the core element of TTRPG, it is in the name after all. Let them discuss, and if needed you let [[Ingo]] be a character who joins in on the fun, take this time to explain everything that has to do with talking and after a while introduce rolling.

> You are all chatting and starting to know each other when a tall figure walks into the room. He walks through the middle of the group onto the stage. And then he just stands there watching to something in the distance.
> (If no one steps out to do something go further. Else if they want to see whether they know the person, let them roll for Perception: 1-10 Don't Recognize, 11-15: Recognition but not where from, 15+: Know it is Ignatius AKA Ingo, who you know from ...)
> All of a sudden you all feel how the room fills itself with a gas and you all feel how you are falling asleep.
> You all wake up with a bunch of random people, you've never seen any of them before and they all look like they are panicking as hard as you are.

## The stone room

The players now roll for the first time. But you are still holding all their character sheets at the moment, so you check how much they rolled and whether it's enough to make a successful check roll.
Let the players roll a perception check.

> 1: You believe that you have always lived in this world and that everything you know was all part of a dream.
> 2-11: You have no clue what happened and are still wondering what the hell is going on.
> 12-18: You now that something is off, and the voices around you sound familiar but you don't know where you know them from.
> 19+: You recognize them from the building you were just in, they look like those people, but still look somewhat different.
> 21+: You also remember inhaling some sort of sleeping gas.

Now the players can decide what they do with their intel they can also investigate the world that you describe.

> After most of the panic is over you finally have time to take in the room. You are in some sort of stone structure with a few windows but in the first instance you see no door. In the middle of the room is a circular table that contains exactly 11 seats. Around you are statues that consist out of ancient armor and at the back of the room is a bookshelf. On the table is a Lamp that is unlit for the moment. In the front of the room there is a big open fire.

The players can now investigate the objects that are named in the above mentioned text.

### Escaping the room

If a player wants to find ways to escape, see this as just escaping, trying to see how to escape from the point they are standing. To see whether they discover anything useful let them roll Insight.

> 1: You think the walls are an illusion and you can run through them.
> 	If they decide to run towards the wall and no one stops them, let them roll a Strength saving throw, if it is lower then their AC, they take a d4 of damage.
> 2-19: You don't really see a way out.
> 20+: You look at the windows and see that something is off about them, you just can't lay your finger on what is going on.

### The windows

If a player goes to the windows and investigate them, tell them that the window is too high up. If they find any way to get up there (a pile of books, a chair, climbing on the bookshelf, a spell, ...) they can reach the window. If the climbing action requires balance in real life, let them do an Acrobatics check.

> 1-10: They fall down
> 11+: They successfully reach the window.

When they reach the window let them do an Arcana check.

> 1+: You look outside and see a beautiful field, the sun stands high in the sky and cows are grazing. The only problem is that there are iron bars before them, which means you can't climb through.
> 18+: But even with this beautiful sight, you still feel that something is off with it, you only can't put your finger on what.

### The table

If a player goes to the table first tell them the following:

> You walk up to the table and see that the wooden seats around the table all have a signature on them, one of the seats has red padding. while the others have no padding at all.

#### The table

If a player wants to investigate more off the table, asks them how they investigate it. 

##### They look at it

> There is nothing, it is just a regular table.

##### They check the table for traps.

Let them roll perception.

> 1-17: The table doesn't seem to be trapped.
> 18+: You go around the table and at the chair with the padding you find a button under the table. 

Then they decide what to do with the button, do they press it?
If they press it:

> The chair with padding sinks into the ground and a panel hides the chair underground. All off a sudden the ancient armor at the end of the room starts moving and looks around at all of you.

Let the players roll Initiative. And roll yourself as well. This is where combat against the armors starts.
![[Animated Armor.png]]
Here you explain the combat mechanism and help players, because a lot is learned by actually playing.

> The armor collapses and the fight seems to be over, the chair that sunk into the ground seems to stay there and there is a sudden silence, and the only thing that changes is the lamp on the table that turns on.

If they decide to investigate the armor that just fell down, read the following.

> Amongst the pile of armor you find a device that looks out off place. Everything here looks like it comes out of a fantasy movie but this device looks very futuristic. It looks like some kind of communication device. You look at the object when there is some text appearing on the device. 
> "Good job, it looks like you are capable enough, run through the fire to get back, I will explain everything when you get back."

### The statues

If they decide to investigate the armor statues they will see nothing special about them.

### The bookshelf

If the players investigate the bookshelf they will find a lot of books with a lot they don't know.

> You look at the bookshelf and see a lot of books. Most of the books look unfamiliar but some ring a bell.

If they went to the table tell them the following.

>There are ten books with a symbol that you remember, they look like the symbols on the chairs.

If they pull out the books with the signs on them:

> The armor explained in the table comes to life.

### The open fire

Ask them what they do.

#### Look at the fire

> Nothing happens

#### Run through the fire

If they defeated the armor they can run through the fire to get back to the hall with the stage. If they did not yet, they will get 1d6 of fire damage.

## The stage

> You all find yourself in the room you where in at the beginning, before you stands the tall figure from the beginning and behind you hear how a portal closes. There is a short silence and the tall person is the first one to break it.
> [[The Creator|Ignatius]]: I understand if you are all confused so let me explain a bit what happened. I am Ignatius, I am the leader of the SAC here in universe RSS-0001. You all have been brought here for a special operation known as project SIMPLE or Secret Intergalactic Multiversal Person Link Element. We try to keep our universe safe by creating bonds with others. SIMPLE mostly takes on expeditions to new territory taking over people from other universes and using them as vessels. We try to keep their lives going by learning more about our vessels before taking them over so we can keep true to their personality while also trying to find their world's SAC and creating alliances with them. And this is why we tested you today, I send you to a universe where we already had vessels prepared for you. But next time it is to you to find out your vessels, but please report to me first to see whether your vessel is suitable for the mission. Is that a bit clear.

The players may now ask some questions, try to answer them to your best capabilities.

> [[The Creator|Ignatius]]: Okay, I guess I should send you back to your universe then. Agents, I see you all in the next mission, be prepared.
