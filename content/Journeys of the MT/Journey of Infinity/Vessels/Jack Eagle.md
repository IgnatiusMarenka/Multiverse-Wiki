### Description
Jack Eagle is a sailor who joined the party on their quest, he has no clue as to why the party is travelling, but he decided to tag along as they seemed like a fun group.

### Level
Level 2 Circle of the Land Druid.


### Personal Quest
- Jack has heard the story of a mysterious jar of mud and is looking to one day find it.
- Jack has met the love of his life on one of his trips and hopes to one day meet again.

### Backstory
Jack was born as a Half-Elf.

>**Fey Ancestry**
>You have advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put you to sleep.

With both his parents refusing him he was forced on the streets from a young age. When he turned 14 they put him on the street for good. Luckily a kind captain found the boy on the streets and invited him to his ship.
The captain decided to keep him on one condition, Jack would need to help with daily tasks. When the ship one day got struck by lightning, he was stranded with the rest of the crew. It was close to night so they decided to send Jack out for exploration because of his great vision in the dark.

>**Darkvision**
>Thanks to your elf blood, you have superior vision in dark en dim conditions. You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

When Jack came back to where he left the crew only to discover they were gone. Luckily Jack had picked up a lot of survival skills while he was on the boat and he soon discovered some footprints in the sand. After following the footprints, he discovers a hidden civilization in the woods. He soon found his friends in a prison made of iron. Death set on freeing his friends he went looking for where everyone went. Soon he found a group of bandits fighting some Druids. From what his captain and also by now friend taught him he knew that it where the bandits that captured his friends.

>**Armor Proficiency**
>Druids will not wear armor or use shields that are made of metal.

After helping the Druids defeat the group of bandits they decided to recruit Jack to become a Druid. They helped him free his friends and Jack said one last goodbye. The druids trained Jack into their practices as druids from the circle of the land. They knew most about the coast but learned him some general stuff as well.
>**Circle Spells: Coast**
>You became a druid on the coast.

>**Circle Spells**
>Your mystical connection to the land infuses you with the ability to cast certain spells. At 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th level you gain access to circle spells connected to the land where you became a druid. Choose that land--arctic, coast, desert, forest, grassland, mountain, swamp, or Underdark--and consult the associated list of spells.
>Once you gain access to a circle spell, you always have it prepared, and it doesn't count against the number of spells you can prepare each day. If you gain access to a spell that doesn't appear on the druid spell list, the spell is nonetheless a druid spell for you.

>**Ritual Casting**
>You can cast a druid spell as a ritual if that spell has the ritual tag and you have the spell prepared.

>**Druidic**
>You know Druidic, the secret language of druids. You can speak the language and use it to leave hidden messages. You and others who know this language automatically spot such a message. Others spot the message's presence with a successful DC 15 Wisdom (Perception) check but can't decipher it without magic.

After some time Jack discovered he started to miss the sea and so he decided to go. He said goodbye to the druids and go on his way. Jack has then traveled the seas for a long time and went many places. He was a transporter of goods and has made a lot of friends along the way.

>**Ship's Passage**

Because you're calling in a favor, you can't be certain of a schedule or route that will meet your every need. Your Dungeon Master will determine how long it takes to get where you need to go.
