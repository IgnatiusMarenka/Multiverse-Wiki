### Description
For more info on *Dungeons & Dragons* click [here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeons_%26_Dragons).
Dungeons and Dragons is a tabletop role playing game, that can be found across the multiverse, it is mostly played in [[The Creator (R-00)|The Creator]] and [[The Nexus (R-01)|The Nexus]]. Although it isn't uncommon to see someone play it in the RS-Multiverse or the RH-Multiverse.
Rare cases of people playing DnD can be found in the RG-Multiverse and the RM-Multiverse but these don't happen too much.
