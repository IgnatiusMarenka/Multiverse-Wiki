# Description
The Secret Agent Company is an organization that focuses on saving big problems. They mostly keep the Universe save and make sure that no major destruction is caused.

# The Buildings
The Buildings and their locations.

## The Headquarters
The HQ is the main building of the SAC, standing 540 meter tall, this is the tallest building of all the SAC buildings. It is here that the leader of the SAC sits.
It is also where [[The Creator]] learned about the multiverse.

### Main Hall
The Main Hall is where everyone enters through, there is a reception here and a mission board for agents to accept. There is also a portal here which offers travelers from distant places a safe way home.

#### The Mission Board
This giant board is linked to a central database and displays available missions. The most important ones are the ones displayed on the board. Specific ones, which are destined for you can be reached with your personal badge.

#### The Portal
This purple glowing oval can bring you to any place, it is activated through the reception who set it's destination. Misuse of the portal can result in getting you fired. Also don't step in it until you have a go from the reception, as the result may not be pretty if you walk through it too early.

# Projects
The currently globally running projects of the Secret Agents Company.

## Project S.I.M.P.L.E.
The Secret Intergalactic Multiversal Person Link Element or SIMPLE is the Multiversal division of the SAC. They make sure order is kept in the multiverse and keep the bonds with all the other universes.
They also scout ahead to see if universes are safe to handle in or not. They recently found a way to do this more efficiently.
