### Description
The Change or "Fenrir Ignatius Maréchal" is the only [[Infinity-Relative]] who is not an Ignatius Marenka Maréchal variant, he is actually the grandson of a former Infinity-Relative called [[The Wise]]. Opposing to other Infinity-Relatives The Change didn't get his powers by Infinity's hands but actually by inheriting them from "The Wise". Because of his visions that were different from the other Relatives, it was Fenrir who got the title of [[Infinity]] after the battle with [[The Origin]].
