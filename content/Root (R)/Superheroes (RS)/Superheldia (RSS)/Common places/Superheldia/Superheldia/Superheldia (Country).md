### Description
Superheldia also known as Terado is the biggest country of [[Superheldia (planet)]]. It is inhabited by [[Superheldians]] since after the war between the [[Vampire Faction]] and the [[Dragon Faction]]. The capital of Terado is [[Newby]], a big city based around the [[Secret Agent Company#The Headquarters|SAC Headquarters]].
