### Description
This Ignatius Marenka Maréchal variant from universe RSS-0001, was the mortal resurrection of the first [[Infinity]]. He was the one who invented and shaped [[Infinity-Relative|The Infinity-Relatives]]. His godly form Infinity is as old as time itself and created the Superheldian Multiverse mainly by himself. After a while he discovered he couldn't handle all the problems of the multiverse himself, that was when he created the Infinity-Relatives. 
After the first fight with [[The Origin]]. He decided to disband the Infinity-Relatives and started protecting the multiverse all by himself. After resurrecting in this knew body, he lost all his memories as a god and hereby forgot about the existence of [[The Origin]]. The gods tried to kill Ignatius multiple times after the resurrection, because they needed their leader with them. Unfortunately there was still a part of Ignatius that was godly which caused many of their killing attempts to fail. In the end when Ingo was in his fifth year of high school [[Ka Ripa]] found a way to kill Ignatius by abducting some of his friends and trading his life for theirs. When he was back, he regained the power to teleport through the multiverse but he still could not remember everything. After regaining part of his memories throughout the years, he took on the title and tasks of Infinity more and more. He and his lover [[Lisa Styler|Lisa]] (who is also the goddess [[Legacy]] in this universe) will wed and get a son, whom they call [[Miro Maréchal|Miro]]. After the birth of their son Miro, who is a demi-god, he and Lisa will found [[Teraci]] to protect the human world from arising threads. One day a villain decides he wants to kill off the whole planet but Teraci can prevent this two times, with the second time having Ignatius sacrifice himself by destroying the stones of Infinity. During the destruction of the stones he binds himself to [[Darc Shadow]] and together they searched for a new host to eventually find [[The Leader]] which he only up to this point talked to using Multiversal telepathy. After regrowing and duplicating the stones within The Leader, he decided to let The Leader keep his stones when he splits his body from The Leader's. This also marked the start of a new generation of Infinity-Relatives. Soon they added more Relatives and Infinity started regaining memories, including the fact that The Origin was somewhere locked up. When he found the capsule, it was too late and The Origin escaped. After defeating The Origin for good, he decided to pass on the cape of Infinity to [[The Change]]. While The Creator still kept an eye on Infinity business to help "The Change" he focused more on [[Secret Agent Company|The S.A.C.]] and their biggest project, [[Secret Agent Company#Project S.I.M.P.L.E.|Project S.I.M.P.L.E.]]
